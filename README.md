# RevisionRoom

RevisionRoom is a streamlined video feedback and approval platform designed to bridge the gap between creative editors and clients. By replacing scattered email chains and vague timeline references with frame-accurate timestamped comments and synchronized version-by-version player comparisons, it provides a centralized, private room where revisions are logged, discussed, and finalized.

Targeted primarily at video editors, freelance creators, and agency production teams, the platform acts as the single source of truth for all post-production review cycles. When a draft is ready, editors invite clients via a secure, passwordless access link. Clients play the video directly in their simplified portal; as soon as they type feedback, the player automatically pauses to capture the exact timestamp, keeping comments tied to precise frames. When the editor addresses the checklist, clients can review and approve the draft, closing the loop with a formal, downloadable approval certificate.

By eliminating communication friction, preventing redundant edits, and providing a clean audit trail of client sign-offs, RevisionRoom transforms post-production from a series of scattered guesses into a clear, unified, and professional handshake.
